CONTROLLER AUTOFIRE

SETUP (once)
1. Put your GameSir in PC/Xbox mode.
2. Double-click "1 - Setup (run once).bat" and approve the prompts.
3. Restart your PC.
4. Open "HidHide Configuration Client" from the Start menu:
   - Applications tab: click +, add your python.exe
     (find it by running "where python" in Command Prompt)
   - Devices tab: tick your GameSir controller
   - Tick "Enable device hiding"

EVERY TIME YOU PLAY
1. Double-click "2 - Start Autofire.bat"
2. Launch the game. Hold RT to auto-fire.

IN-GAME (hold BACK/View + D-pad)
  Up    = auto-fire on/off
  Right = faster, Left = slower
  Down  = also auto-fire RB

To use your controller normally without the script, untick
"Enable device hiding" in HidHide.

THE LAST OF US PART II: your trainer's Rapid Fire (Num 8) already does
this, so you don't need the script for that game.

BLACK OPS 3: in Steam, right-click the game > Properties > Controller,
set Steam Input to "Disable". Campaign / Zombies only, not online.
